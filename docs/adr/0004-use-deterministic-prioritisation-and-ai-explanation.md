# Use deterministic prioritisation and AI explanation

The Priority Queue will be calculated from visible factors such as time urgency, threshold status, client impact, objective mismatch, and evidence confidence. AI may explain the result and draft a Conversation Plan, but it will not determine an opaque ranking or execute an Advisory Action. Each Conversation Plan will expose its evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, opening question, options, and RM controls so the reasoning remains reviewable and the RM retains responsibility.
