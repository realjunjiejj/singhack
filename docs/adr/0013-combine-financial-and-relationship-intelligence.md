# Combine financial and relationship intelligence

JB Clarity will surface Anticipatory Signals such as collateral deterioration, approaching tax or liquidity needs, redemption gates, mandate breaches, and a Governance Clock. It will also surface source-cited Open Loops from RM notes as candidates for Priscilla to confirm and resolve. Both signal types feed one Client Case and one Meeting Brief, allowing the RM to anticipate what the client may notice, preserve relationship continuity, and prepare the next conversation without treating unstructured notes as settled facts.
