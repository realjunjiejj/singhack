## Full Presentation Script

### Minute 1 (0:00 – 1:00) · The Morning Panic & Deterministic Prioritization

**[STAGE ACTION (0:00 – 0:15):**  
*Presenter 2 (Priscilla) sits or stands by the laptop pretending to frantically sift through stacks of papers and browser tabs, rubbing her temples in stress.*  
*Presenter 3 holds a phone to ear, tapping Presenter 2's shoulder repeatedly mimicking incoming notifications: “Ping! LTV alert in Jakarta! Ping! Did you check the ESG audit?! What about my bonds?!”*  
*Presenter 2 throws her hands up in overwhelm.* **]**

**PRESENTER 1 (Speaks with strong projection):**
> “Hey Judges, meet Priscilla Ong. Senior Relationship Manager at Julius Baer.
>
> It’s 8:30 AM. Priscilla has 20 high net-worth clients, 100 million dollars under management, and three voicemails.
>
> In private banking, Priscilla’s biggest enemy isn’t a lack of data—it’s **cognitive overload**. She spends four hours every day digging through spreadsheets, dreading the one hidden margin breach or missed mandate deadline that could blow up her career.
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenter 2 clicks the screen to show the Clarity Priority Queue — instantly relaxes, exhales, and smiles]**
>
> This is **Julius Bär Clarity**. In three seconds, it eliminates Priscilla’s morning panic.
>
> Our deterministic AI engine analyzed all the data she provided overnight. Look at who is at the top of her priority list: **Aishah binti Rahman**.
>
> Priscilla doesn’t have to guess who to call first. Clarity has detected two pressing issues: a contractually binding mandate breach of 5.6 million dollars in thermal energy holdings, as well as an urgent 1.45-million Australian dollar tuition payment due in just six days. This definitely requires Priscilla’s immediate attention.
>
> Just like that, with Clarity’s help, Priscilla's morning prioritization is done before she takes her first sip of coffee.”

---

### Minute 2 (1:00 – 2:00) · The Difficult Client: From Standoff to Solution

**[STAGE ACTION (1:00 – 1:12):**  
*Presenter 2 (Priscilla) clicks the **[Cheung]** button on screen.*  
*Presenter 3 (Jovan) steps forward, crossing his arms with a stern, stubborn expression, acting as the 71-year-old client Mr. Cheung.*  
*Presenter 3 (Jovan - gruff, upset tone):*  
**“Priscilla! My bond portfolio is down five million dollars! I forbid you from selling anything at a loss! I’m waiting until they get back to par!”**  
*Presenter 2 smiles calmly, gesturing toward Clarity's Executive AI Analysis card on screen.* **]**

**PRESENTER 2 (Speaks with empathy and confidence):**
> “Every RM has a client like Mr. Cheung. He is 71, retired, and drawing 1.1 million dollars a year for daily living and medical care.
>
> If Priscilla simply told him 'the bank recommends taking a loss', he’d fire her on the spot.
>
> But look at what Clarity’s **Executive AI Advisory Analysis** suggests for Priscilla:
>
> Mr. Cheung’s longest bond—the US Treasury 2.375%—doesn't mature until **2045**, when he will be **90 years old**. Waiting nineteen years to break even while draining over a million dollars a year is mathematically impossible.
>
> Clarity not only highlights the potential risks in Mr. Cheung’s portfolio, but it also prepares a template for Priscilla such that she would be able to discuss this issue tactfully with Mr. Cheung.
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenter 2 turns to Presenter 3 (Jovan), delivering the script warmly]:**  
> *‘Mr. Cheung...’* *(pause)*  
>
> **Presenter 3 (Jovan, still skeptical):**  
> *“Yes...?”*  
>
> **Presenter 2:**  
> *“I completely agree we shouldn't fire-sell your 2045 Treasuries. But waiting 19 years doesn't fit your immediate 1.1 million dollar medical needs. Let’s lock in today's high 4.8% short-term yields for your next 3 years of cashflow so your living care is 100% guaranteed, and we will let your 2045 Treasuries sit untouched as a long-term inheritance for your children.”*  
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenter 3 (Jovan) uncrosses arms, face lights up with relief and nods]:**  
> **Presenter 3 (Jovan):**  
> *“I guess you are right, thank you for your insights!”* **]**

---

### Minute 3 (2:00 – 3:00) · Auditable Evidence, Portfolio Charts & The Finish

**[STAGE ACTION (2:00 – 2:05):**  
*Presenter 3 steps back into his presenter role with executive presence.*  
*Presenter 2 hovers the mouse over the blue evidence citation box in Cheung's case.* **]**

**PRESENTER 3 (Speaks with authority):**
> “When it comes to wealthy clients with huge portfolios, Priscilla cannot afford to make a single mistake. This then leads us to an important question: **How does Priscilla know that the information Julius Bär Clarity provides is accurate?**
>
> With Clarity, every insight is anchored by an auditable **Evidence Packet**. Every percentage, transaction date, and duration metric is directly linked to verified source records. 
> 
> Let’s take Mr. Cheung’s bonds for example. Watch what happens when Priscilla clicks this blue evidence box.
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenter 2 clicks the blue evidence box on screen — the Evidence Chain drawer immediately slides open on the right side!]**
>
> As shown on the right side of the screen, clicking the blue box instantly opens the **Evidence Chain** drawer! All evidence used for analysis is drawn directly from the CSV or Excel files that have been uploaded by Priscilla. This ensures that she will be able to properly utilize company records to provide accurate and reliable data for the analysis. The particular values extracted from the uploaded files are included as well, allowing Priscilla to verify the information conveniently.
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenter 2 closes or pans over to the Portfolio Trend and Asset Allocation charts]**
>
> Clarity then connects these verified records directly into dynamic **Portfolio Charts**—giving Priscilla an immediate visual breakdown of Mr. Cheung’s heavy fixed income concentration and historical yield trends alongside risk thresholds.
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenter 2 scrolls down, clicks 'Prepare Conversation' and then clicks 'Approve']**
>
> With one final click, Priscilla prepares and approves an audit-ready **Meeting Brief**, ready for her client call.
>
> **[STAGE ACTION: Presenters 1, 2, and 3 stand together facing the judges for the short, punchy ending]**
>
> **PRESENTER 3 (or all 3 in strong unison):**
> “Julius Bär Clarity turns four hours of spreadsheet dread into fifteen minutes of trusted client advice—with the Relationship Manager always in control.
>
> Thank you!”
