# Julius Bär Clarity — 3-Minute, 3-Presenter Pitch & Roleplay Script
## "A Day in the Life of Priscilla Ong"

> **Concept:** Dynamic, high-energy pitch combining interactive acting with live UI interaction.  
> **Characters:**  
> - **Presenter 1 (Narrator & Tech Lead):** Delivers the opening hook and architecture/impact finale.  
> - **Presenter 2 (Priscilla Ong, RM):** Acts as Priscilla in Minute 1, delivers Advisory deep-dive in Minute 2.  
> - **Presenter 3 (Client Mr. Cheung / Compliance):** Acts as stressed/stubborn client, delivers Governance & Evidence in Minute 3.  
> **Total Time:** 180 Seconds (3:00) · 1 Laptop on `http://127.0.0.1:3000` with mouse operated by Presenter 2.

---


## Full Presentation Script

### Minute 1 (0:00 – 1:00) · Priscilla's Morning Chaos vs. The Priority Queue

**[STAGE ACTION (0:00 – 0:15):**  
*Presenter 2 (Priscilla) sits/stands at the laptop pretending to drown in 20 browser tabs, rubbing temples frantically.*  
*Presenter 3 holds a phone to ear, tapping Presenter 2 on the shoulder loudly mimicking incoming alerts: "Ping! LTV alert in Jakarta! Ping! Did you check the ESG audit?! What about my bonds?!"*  
*Presenter 2 throws hands up in despair.* **]**

**PRESENTER 1 (Speaks with strong projection):**
> “Meet Priscilla Ong. Senior Relationship Manager at Julius Baer.
>
> It’s 8:30 AM. Priscilla has 20 ultra-high-net-worth clients, 40 million dollars under management, and three voicemails. 
> 
> In private banking, Priscilla’s biggest enemy isn’t a lack of data—it’s **cognitive overload**. She spends four hours every day digging through spreadsheets, dreading the one hidden margin breach or missed mandate deadline that could blow up her career.
>
> **[Presenter 2 clicks screen to show Clarity full Priority Queue — instantly relaxes and smiles]**
>
> This is **Julius Bär Clarity**. In three seconds, it eliminates Priscilla’s morning panic.
>
> Our deterministic multi-agent ranking engine analyzed her entire book overnight. Look at Number One: **Aishah binti Rahman**.
>
> Priscilla doesn’t have to guess who to call first. Clarity detected a dual-trigger collision: a contractually binding mandate breach of 5.6 million dollars in thermal energy holdings, colliding with an urgent 1.45-million Australian dollar tuition payment due in just six days.
>
> Urgency separated from confidence. Priscilla's morning prioritization is done before she takes her first sip of coffee.”

---

### Minute 2 (1:00 – 2:00) · The Difficult Client: From Standoff to Solution

**[STAGE ACTION (1:00 – 1:15):**  
*Presenter 2 (Priscilla) clicks the **[Cheung]** button on screen.*  
*Presenter 3 steps forward, crossing arms, acting as the stubborn, wealthy 71-year-old client Mr. Cheung.*  
*Presenter 3 (Cheung - in character, gruff voice): "Priscilla! My bond portfolio is down five million dollars! I forbid you from selling anything at a loss! I’m waiting until they get back to par!"*  
*Presenter 2 smiles calmly, pointing to Clarity's Executive AI Analysis card on screen.* **]**

**PRESENTER 2 (Speaks with empathy and confidence):**
> “Every RM has a client like Mr. Cheung. He is 71, retired, and drawing 1.1 million dollars a year for daily living and medical care.
>
> If Priscilla simply told him 'the bank recommends taking a loss', he’d fire her on the spot.
>
> But look at what Clarity’s **Executive AI Advisory Analysis** surfaces for Priscilla:
>
> Mr. Cheung’s longest bond—the US Treasury 2.375%—doesn't mature until **2045**, when he will be **90 years old**. Waiting nineteen years to break even while draining over a million dollars a year is mathematically impossible.
>
> Clarity doesn't just show the trap—it gives Priscilla the exact psychological script to guide him:
>
> **[Presenter 2 turns to Presenter 3 (Cheung), delivering the script warmly]:**
> *‘Mr. Cheung, I completely agree we shouldn't fire-sell your 2045 Treasuries. But waiting 19 years doesn't fit your immediate 1.1 million dollar medical needs. Let’s lock in today's high 4.8% short-term yields for your next 3 years of cashflow so your living care is 100% guaranteed, and we will let your 2045 Treasuries sit untouched as a long-term inheritance for your children.’*
>
> **[STAGE ACTION:** *Presenter 3's arms uncross, expression softens from stubborn to delighted: "Wait... guaranteed care and an inheritance for the kids? Priscilla, let's do that."* **]**
>
> Priscilla just turned a hostile client standoff into a winning advisory relationship.”

---

### Minute 3 (2:00 – 3:00) · Auditable Evidence, 1-Click Governance & Peace of Mind

**[STAGE ACTION (2:00 – 2:15):**  
*Presenter 2 expands the **Evidence Packet** on screen, showing cited source records, then scrolls to the Work Surface.*  
*Presenter 3 steps forward into Presenter role.* **]**

**PRESENTER 3 (Speaks with authority):**
> “How does Priscilla know she can trust this?
>
> In private banking, hallucinated advice means compliance penalties. With Clarity, every insight is anchored by an auditable **Evidence Packet**. Every percentage, transaction date, and duration metric links directly to verified source records.
>
> **[Presenter 2 clicks 'Prepare Conversation' on screen, then clicks 'Approve']**
>
> And Priscilla is always in full control. Clarity drafts the structured Meeting Brief and client-ready bilingual notes in seconds—saving her two hours of manual write-ups.
>
> She reviews the brief, modifies it if desired, and clicks **Approve**. If portfolio data changes tomorrow, that approval safely invalidates, ensuring bank-grade auditability.
>
> Nothing is ever traded or sent without Priscilla's explicit sign-off.”

**PRESENTER 1 (Steps forward to close):**
> “Our engine operates deterministically and works 100% offline, keeping sensitive client wealth data inside Julius Baer’s secure perimeter.
>
> For Priscilla, Julius Bär Clarity means no more late-night spreadsheets, no more fear of missing regulatory breaches, and confident, trusted conversations every single day.
>
> Technology that doesn't replace the Relationship Manager—it makes them indispensable.
>
> Thank you.”

## Presenter Rehearsal Tips

1. **Acting Energy:**
   - **Presenter 2 (Priscilla)** should visibly transition from **stressed/overwhelmed** in Minute 1 to **calm, empowered, and in control** in Minutes 2 and 3.
   - **Presenter 3 (Cheung roleplay)** should make the stubborn objection snappy (under 10 seconds): cross arms, lean forward, speak with genuine conviction (*"I forbid you from selling at a loss!"*). When Priscilla responds, nod with genuine realization.
2. **Timing Breakdown:**
   - `0:00 - 0:15`: Acting Priscilla's chaos.
   - `0:15 - 1:00`: Presenter 1 pitch + Aishah #1.
   - `1:00 - 1:12`: Presenter 3 mini-skit (Cheung objection).
   - `1:12 - 1:55`: Presenter 2 explains duration dilemma + delivers script.
   - `1:55 - 2:00`: Presenter 3 reaction ("Let's do that!").
   - `2:00 - 2:40`: Presenter 3 on Evidence + 1-Click Approval.
   - `2:40 - 3:00`: Presenter 1 closing punchline & thank you.
3. **Screen Control (Presenter 2):**
   - Minute 1: Stay on full Priority Queue.
   - Minute 2: Single click on `[Cheung]`, scroll down to Executive AI Analysis.
   - Minute 3: Click `[Evidence]`, scroll down to click `[Prepare conversation]` then `[Approve]`.
