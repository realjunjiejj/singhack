## Rehearsal & Delivery Guidelines

1. **Pacing & Word Count:**
   - Total spoken words: ~380 words across 3 minutes (~125 words per presenter).
   - This allows 10–15 seconds per minute for clicking, screen transitions, and natural pauses.
2. **Mouse Operator Discipline:**
   - Stay smooth. Don't scroll erratically.
   - At `0:00`: Full queue visible, mouse hovers on Aishah.
   - At `1:00`: One clean click on `[Cheung]`. Smooth scroll to the Executive AI card.
   - At `2:00`: Expand the Evidence dropdown, then scroll down to click `[Prepare conversation]` and `[Approve]`.
3. **Key Numbers to Memorize (Zero Guessing):**
   - **Cheung:** Age 71, USD 1.1M drawdown, down USD 5.8M on fixed income, 2045 maturity (19 years / age 90), 4.8% short-term yields.
   - **Aishah:** Sustainable Balanced mandate, SGD 5.6M non-compliant holdings, AUD 1.45M tuition due in 6 days.
   - **Book:** 20 clients, as of 26 August 2026.
